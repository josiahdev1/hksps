# Later

Good ideas that are not in the MVP.

Parking something here is a decision, not a loss. It is how the MVP stays small enough to actually ship by Session 3.

- **Real-Time Stopwatch & Clock-In/Out Timer**: Live in-browser stopwatch with background persist, elapsed-time heartbeats, and geolocation verification.
- **QR Code & Beacons**: On-site scanning for automated check-in and check-out at department desks.
- **Scholarship Renewal Workflow**: Self-service renewal submission, document upload, and coordinator decision queue.
- **Eligibility & Warning Tags**: Automated tagging of at-risk scholars and administrative hold tags.
- **Export & Reports Engine**: PDF completion certificates with digital seal, CSV/Excel ledger export for registrar auditing.
- **Analytics & Visual Dashboards**: Graphical distribution charts (hours by department, completion velocity over the term).
- **External Notifications**: SMS alerts for urgent duty slots and automated email summaries.
- **Roster & Bulk Import**: Bulk CSV ingestion of scholar rosters, IDs, and initial hour quotas.
- **Multi-Campus Multi-Tenancy**: Cross-campus department management and role federation.
